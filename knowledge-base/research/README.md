# Research and Reference Materials

Store durable technical research that supports hardware compatibility, software selection, model evaluation, protocols, integrations, and engineering decisions.

Each contribution must record the research question or claim; source title, author/publisher, and URL or repository reference; source type; publication/revision date and retrieval date; applicable hardware/software versions; findings and limitations; verification status and reviewer; and related equipment, projects, issues, and decisions.

Distinguish primary sources from secondary commentary. Do not promote an unsupported claim to an approved engineering fact.
